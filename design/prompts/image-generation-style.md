Use this as optional creative guidance, not an instruction to generate an asset:

> Calm botanical knowledge garden on a deep green-black ground. A small growing
> seedling, quiet orbital geometry, restrained luminous green linework and generous
> negative space. Thin architectural traces, subtle surface glow, pale readable
> typography. Keep the composition focused and technically precise, with organic
> growth as the central metaphor. Avoid clutter and bright effects behind text.

Use the current token palette and the visual reference. Keep public identity
